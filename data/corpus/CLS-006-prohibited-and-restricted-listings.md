# Prohibited and Restricted Listing Categories

## 1. Definitions

### CLS-006-C1 Prohibited Versus Restricted
A prohibited item may not be listed under any circumstance. A restricted item may be
listed only by a seller holding the applicable category approval and only where every
condition attached to that approval is met.

## 2. Prohibited Items

### CLS-006-C2 Categorically Prohibited Goods
The following may not be listed: weapons and ammunition, explosive precursors,
counterfeit goods, recalled products, human remains or bodily fluids, stolen goods,
government identity documents, live animals, and any item whose supply is prohibited by
law in the storefront's jurisdiction.

### CLS-006-C3 Recalled Product Rule
A product subject to an active safety recall in the storefront jurisdiction is
prohibited from the moment the recall is published, regardless of the seller's stock
position or the recall's remediation status.

## 3. Restricted Categories Requiring Approval

### CLS-006-C4 Approval-Gated Categories
Category approval is required before listing in: dietary supplements, cosmetics and
personal care, children's toys and juvenile products, electrical goods with a mains
connection, laser products, battery-powered products containing lithium cells, and
chemical or cleaning products.

### CLS-006-C5 Approval Evidence
An approval application shall provide the product's compliance documentation, a
supplier declaration of conformity where applicable, and images of the physical product
labelling as it will be supplied to the buyer.

### CLS-006-C6 Approval Scope
Category approval is granted per seller per category, not per listing, and does not
transfer between marketplace accounts or across a change of legal entity.

### CLS-006-C7 Approval Withdrawal
An approval may be withdrawn where the seller's category defect rate exceeds 5 percent
over a rolling 60-day window or where a Tier 1 safety defect is confirmed.

## 4. Interaction With Other Policies

### CLS-006-C8 Precedence
Where this document conflicts with a category guideline, the more restrictive provision
applies. Restricted-substance rules in the RSR series apply independently and are never
waived by a category approval.

### CLS-006-C9 Grey Area Handling
Where it is unclear whether an item falls within a prohibited class, the seller shall
seek a written determination before listing. Listing first and seeking clarification
afterward is treated as an unapproved listing.

### CLS-006-C10 No Legal Advice
Determinations under this document reflect marketplace policy only and do not
constitute legal advice on the lawfulness of supplying the item in any jurisdiction.
