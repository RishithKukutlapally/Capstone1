# Consumer Electronics Category Guideline

## 1. Scope

### CAT-001-C1 Covered Products
This guideline covers audio equipment, televisions and displays, computing hardware,
mobile devices, wearables, cameras, and consumer networking equipment. Products
containing lithium cells are additionally governed by SAF-003.

## 2. Mandatory Category Attributes

### CAT-001-C2 Required Attributes
In addition to the platform-wide mandatory set in CLS-003-C1, electronics listings shall
declare: input voltage range, plug type, connectivity standards supported, warranty
period in months, and whether the item is new, refurbished, or open-box.

### CAT-001-C3 Condition Declaration
Refurbished items shall declare the refurbisher and the functional test performed.
Open-box items shall state whether the original accessories are complete. Misdeclaring
condition is a Tier 1 defect.

### CAT-001-C4 Compatibility Claims
Claimed compatibility with a named third-party device shall be limited to devices the
seller has verified. Blanket claims such as "works with all smartphones" are prohibited
under CLS-002-C9.

## 3. Electrical Safety

### CAT-001-C5 Mains-Powered Product Approval
Any product with a mains connection requires the electrical goods category approval
described in CLS-006-C4. The approval application shall include the plug and adapter
specification for each storefront the product will be sold into.

### CAT-001-C6 Plug Compatibility Labelling
Where the supplied plug does not match the destination storefront's socket standard, the
listing shall state this prominently in the first bullet feature and shall state whether
an adapter is included. Omission is a Tier 1 safety-labelling defect.

### CAT-001-C7 Energy Labelling
Displays, and any product subject to a statutory energy label in the storefront
jurisdiction, shall carry the energy efficiency class attribute and an image of the
energy label as a secondary product image.

## 4. Wireless and Radio

### CAT-001-C8 Radio Equipment Declaration
Products transmitting on licensed or licence-exempt radio spectrum shall declare the
frequency bands used and the maximum radiated power, and shall hold the applicable radio
equipment conformity marking for the storefront jurisdiction.

### CAT-001-C9 Signal Boosters and Jammers
Signal jammers are prohibited under CLS-006-C2. Cellular signal boosters are restricted
and require carrier authorisation evidence with the category approval.

### CAT-001-C10 Data and Privacy Features
Products with a camera or always-on microphone shall disclose that capability in the
bullet features and shall state whether recordings are stored on-device or in a cloud
service.
