# Dangerous Goods Classification and Transport Requirements

## 1. Classification

### SAF-004-C1 Definition of Dangerous Goods
Dangerous goods are articles or substances capable of posing a risk to health, safety,
property, or the environment during transport, and which are classified under the
applicable transport regulations.

### SAF-004-C2 Common Marketplace Classes
The classes most frequently encountered are: flammable liquids such as perfumes,
solvents and nail products; flammable solids; oxidising substances; toxic substances;
corrosive substances such as drain cleaners and battery acid; lithium batteries; and
aerosols under pressure.

### SAF-004-C3 Seller Classification Duty
The seller is responsible for correctly classifying the product before listing. Where
the seller is uncertain, the safety data sheet section 14 provides the transport
classification.

## 2. Declaration Obligations

### SAF-004-C4 Mandatory Fulfilment Flag
Every dangerous good shall carry the hazardous goods fulfilment flag with the UN number,
proper shipping name, class, and packing group where assigned. Omission is a Tier 1
defect under CLS-004-C8 and blocks fulfilment-centre intake.

### SAF-004-C5 Limited Quantity and Excepted Quantity
Where a product ships under a limited quantity or excepted quantity provision, the
listing shall record the provision relied upon and the per-package quantity limit.

### SAF-004-C6 Air Transport Restrictions
Products forbidden for passenger aircraft transport shall be flagged as surface-only.
Listings without this flag may be routed to air freight, which is a transport safety
breach attributable to the seller.

## 3. Packaging and Labelling

### SAF-004-C7 Outer Packaging Marks
Dangerous goods shall be supplied in packaging bearing the applicable hazard label,
orientation arrows where required, and the UN specification marking where the packing
group requires performance-tested packaging.

### SAF-004-C8 Overpack Rules
Where multiple dangerous goods are consolidated, the overpack shall reproduce every
applicable hazard label and shall be marked "OVERPACK".

### SAF-004-C9 Quantity Limits Per Consignment
Consumer consignments shall not exceed the marketplace's per-consignment net quantity
limit for the class. Splitting a single order across consignments to evade the limit is
prohibited.

### SAF-004-C10 Prohibited Transport Items
Explosives, infectious substances, radioactive material, and self-reactive substances
may not be listed and are prohibited under CLS-006-C2.
