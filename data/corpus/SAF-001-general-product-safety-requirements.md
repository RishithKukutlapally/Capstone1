# General Product Safety Requirements

## 1. General Obligation

### SAF-001-C1 Safe Product Obligation
Every product offered shall be a safe product: under normal or reasonably foreseeable
conditions of use, including duration of use and the needs of vulnerable user groups, it
shall present no risk or only the minimum risk compatible with the product's use.

### SAF-001-C2 Reasonably Foreseeable Misuse
Assessment of safety shall account for reasonably foreseeable misuse, particularly by
children, older users, and users with disabilities, and not only for use strictly in
accordance with the instructions.

## 2. Economic Operator Identification

### SAF-001-C3 Responsible Person Requirement
Each listing shall identify an economic operator established in the storefront
jurisdiction who is responsible for the product's compliance. The operator's name and
postal address shall appear on the product, on its packaging, or in an accompanying
document.

### SAF-001-C4 Listing Disclosure of Operator
The economic operator's name and contact address shall be reproduced in the listing's
compliance attribute fields. Absence of this information is a Tier 1 defect for any
product in an approval-gated category.

## 3. Instructions and Warnings

### SAF-001-C5 Instructions Requirement
Products shall be supplied with instructions and safety information in the official
language of the storefront jurisdiction. Instructions supplied only in a foreign
language are a Tier 1 defect.

### SAF-001-C6 Warning Prominence
Safety warnings shall be legible, indelible, and reproduced in the listing where the
warning concerns a hazard that would influence a purchase decision, including choking,
strangulation, burn, ingestion, electrical, and chemical hazards.

### SAF-001-C7 Warning Placement in Listings
A required safety warning shall appear within the first three bullet features and shall
not be placed solely in the long description or solely in an image.

## 4. Traceability and Recall

### SAF-001-C8 Batch Traceability
Products shall carry a type, batch, serial, or model reference enabling identification.
Where the product is too small, the reference shall appear on the packaging.

### SAF-001-C9 Recall Cooperation
On notification of a safety issue, the seller shall within 48 hours suspend the listing,
provide sales volume data for the affected batches, and cooperate with corrective action.

### SAF-001-C10 Incident Reporting
A seller aware that a product it supplied is dangerous shall report the incident through
the marketplace safety portal within 48 hours of becoming aware.
