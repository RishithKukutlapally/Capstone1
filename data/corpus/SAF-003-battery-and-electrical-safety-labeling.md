# Battery and Electrical Safety Labeling Requirements

## 1. Lithium Cells and Batteries

### SAF-003-C1 Declaration Requirement
Any product containing or shipped with a lithium cell or battery shall declare cell
chemistry, cell count, watt-hour rating, and whether the cell is contained in the
equipment, packed with the equipment, or supplied loose.

### SAF-003-C2 Watt-Hour Threshold
Lithium-ion cells exceeding 20 watt-hours per cell, or batteries exceeding 100
watt-hours, are restricted and require transport documentation with the category
approval. Loose lithium-ion cells above 20 watt-hours may not be sold to consumers.

### SAF-003-C3 Transport Classification
Lithium batteries shall be flagged as dangerous goods under CLS-004-C8 with the
applicable transport classification and packing instruction reference.

### SAF-003-C4 Damaged Cell Prohibition
Damaged, swollen, recalled, or reclaimed lithium cells may not be listed under any
circumstance.

## 2. Button and Coin Cells

### SAF-003-C5 Scope of Button Cell Rules
These clauses apply to any consumer product containing a button or coin cell of 32
millimetres diameter or less, including toys, remote controls, greeting cards, novelty
lights, thermometers, and hearing aid accessories.

### SAF-003-C6 Secured Compartment Requirement
The battery compartment shall be secured such that it cannot be opened without a tool,
or requires two independent and simultaneous movements to open. A compartment openable
by hand in a single motion is a Tier 1 safety defect.

### SAF-003-C7 Required Button Cell Warning
The product, its packaging, and the listing shall carry the warning: "WARNING: INGESTION
HAZARD — This product contains a button or coin cell battery. Swallowing can lead to
chemical burns, perforation of soft tissue, and death. Seek immediate medical attention."
The warning shall also state to keep batteries out of reach of children and to dispose of
used batteries immediately.

## 3. Mains Electrical Safety

### SAF-003-C8 Conformity Marking
Mains-powered products shall carry the conformity marking required by the storefront
jurisdiction and shall reference the applicable safety standard in the compliance
attributes.

### SAF-003-C9 Insulation Class and Ingress Rating
Products shall declare their insulation class and, where marketed for outdoor or
bathroom use, their ingress protection rating.

### SAF-003-C10 Charger and Adapter Requirements
Supplied chargers shall bear the conformity marking, shall declare output voltage and
current, and shall not be substituted for a non-original adapter without disclosure in
the listing.
