# Choking, Ingestion and Mechanical Hazard Labeling Requirements

## 1. Small Parts

### SAF-002-C1 Small Part Definition
A small part is any component, including a component that detaches under the standard
use and abuse tests, that fits entirely within a cylinder of 31.7 millimetres diameter
and 57.1 millimetres depth measured at the shallowest point.

### SAF-002-C2 Under 36 Months Prohibition
A toy intended for children under 36 months shall not contain, and shall not be capable
of generating, a small part. Such a product may not be listed for that age group. This
is an absolute prohibition, not a labelling obligation.

### SAF-002-C3 Over 36 Months Labelling Obligation
A toy intended for children of 36 months and over that contains a small part shall carry
a choking hazard warning on the packaging and in the listing.

### SAF-002-C4 Required Choking Warning Text
The warning shall read: "WARNING: CHOKING HAZARD — Small parts. Not for children under
3 years." The signal word shall be in uppercase. The warning shall be accompanied by the
age-restriction symbol where the packaging permits.

## 2. Balloons, Marbles and Small Balls

### SAF-002-C5 Additional Hazard Classes
Latex balloons, marbles, and small balls under 44.5 millimetres carry a mandatory
warning irrespective of the declared age grade. The latex balloon warning shall
additionally state that children under 8 years can choke or suffocate on uninflated or
broken balloons and that adult supervision is required.

## 3. Magnets

### SAF-002-C6 Magnetic Flux Threshold
A loose or detachable magnet, or a magnetic component that is a small part, shall not
have a magnetic flux index of 50 kG²mm² or greater. Products exceeding this threshold
are prohibited in toys and in novelty products marketed to any age group.

### SAF-002-C7 Magnet Ingestion Warning
Where magnets are permitted, the listing shall carry the warning that swallowing more
than one magnet may cause serious or fatal internal injury and that immediate medical
attention is required.

## 4. Other Mechanical Hazards

### SAF-002-C8 Strangulation Hazard
Cords, straps, and loops with a free length exceeding 220 millimetres on a product
intended for children under 3 require a strangulation warning under CAT-003-C8.

### SAF-002-C9 Sharp Edges and Points
Products intended for children shall not present accessible sharp edges or points beyond
those inherent to the product's function, and where inherent, the function shall be
disclosed with an age restriction.

### SAF-002-C10 Expanding Materials
Products containing materials that expand more than 50 percent in any dimension on
contact with water are prohibited where any component is a small part.
