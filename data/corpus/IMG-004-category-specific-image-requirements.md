# Category-Specific Image Requirements

## 1. Apparel and Footwear

### IMG-004-C1 Model and Flat-Lay Rules
Apparel may be shown on a model or as a flat lay. Where a model is used, the model's
height and the garment size worn shall be stated in the image caption or the bullet
features. Mannequins shall be either fully invisible or a neutral, unbranded form.

### IMG-004-C2 Mandatory Apparel Images
Apparel listings shall include a front view, a back view, a fabric close-up, and the
size chart image required by CAT-002-C4.

## 2. Food, Supplements and Cosmetics

### IMG-004-C3 Ingredient and Nutrition Panel Image
Food, supplement, and cosmetic listings shall include a sharp, legible, unretouched image
of the physical ingredient list and, for food, the nutrition panel. This image is
mandatory and its absence causes suppression under CAT-004-C5 and CAT-005-C3.

### IMG-004-C4 Serving Depiction
Where a serving suggestion is depicted, the image shall carry an on-image caption stating
"serving suggestion" and shall not include ingredients not supplied with the product.

## 3. Toys and Juvenile Products

### IMG-004-C5 Warning Label Image
Toys carrying a choking, magnet, or button cell warning shall include a legible image of
the packaging face bearing that warning, per SAF-002-C4 and SAF-003-C7.

### IMG-004-C6 Depiction of Use
Images shall depict age-appropriate use consistent with CAT-003-C10, with any required
supervision visible or noted in the caption.

## 4. Electronics and Chemicals

### IMG-004-C7 Port, Plug and Rating Plate
Electronics listings shall include an image of the supplied plug and, where present, the
rating plate showing input voltage, so buyers can verify compatibility under CAT-001-C6.

### IMG-004-C8 Hazard Pictogram Image
Chemical products shall include an image of the label face bearing the hazard pictograms,
signal word, and precautionary statements required by CAT-006-C3.

### IMG-004-C9 Energy Label Image
Products subject to an energy label under CAT-001-C7 shall include the energy label as a
secondary image in its prescribed graphical form, unaltered.

### IMG-004-C10 Precedence
Where this document and a category guideline both specify an image requirement, both
apply cumulatively; neither waives the other.
