# Restricted Substances Framework and General Limits

## 1. Framework

### RSR-001-C1 Purpose
This framework establishes the substances restricted or prohibited in products sold
through the marketplace, the concentration limits that apply, and the evidence a seller
shall hold.

### RSR-001-C2 Precedence Over Category Approval
A restricted-substance limit applies independently of any category approval. Holding a
category approval never waives a limit in the RSR series, per CLS-006-C8.

### RSR-001-C3 Concentration Basis
Unless a clause states otherwise, a concentration limit is expressed as a weight
percentage of the homogeneous material, not of the finished article.

## 2. General Limits

### RSR-001-C4 Heavy Metals in Consumer Articles
Lead shall not exceed 0.009 percent by weight in any accessible material of a product
intended for children. Cadmium shall not exceed 0.01 percent in plastic materials.
Mercury is prohibited in consumer articles other than as specified in a lighting or
measuring device exemption.

### RSR-001-C5 Phthalates
The sum of the restricted phthalates DEHP, DBP, and BBP shall not exceed 0.1 percent by
weight in any plasticised material. In toys and childcare articles that can be placed in
the mouth, DINP, DIDP, and DNOP are additionally restricted to the same limit.

### RSR-001-C6 Nickel Release
Articles in prolonged contact with skin shall not release more than 0.5 micrograms of
nickel per square centimetre per week. For items inserted into pierced body parts the
limit is 0.2 micrograms per square centimetre per week.

### RSR-001-C7 Formaldehyde
Free formaldehyde in textiles in direct skin contact shall not exceed 75 milligrams per
kilogram, and 30 milligrams per kilogram for articles intended for children under 36
months.

## 3. Evidence

### RSR-001-C8 Test Report Requirement
The seller shall hold a test report from an accredited laboratory demonstrating
compliance with every limit applicable to the product, issued within the preceding 36
months or since the last material change, whichever is more recent.

### RSR-001-C9 Production of Evidence
Test reports shall be produced within 10 business days of a marketplace request. Failure
to produce evidence sustains a non-compliance finding and suppresses the listing.

### RSR-001-C10 Supplier Declarations
A supplier declaration of conformity is accepted as supporting evidence but does not
substitute for a test report where a clause expressly requires one.
