# Food, Beverage and Dietary Supplements Category Guideline

## 1. Scope and Approval

### CAT-004-C1 Covered Products
This guideline covers packaged food, beverages, dietary supplements, sports nutrition,
and infant formula. Dietary supplements require the category approval in CLS-006-C4.

### CAT-004-C2 Infant Formula Restriction
Infant formula for children under 12 months is a restricted product requiring a separate
approval and may not be listed by third-party sellers without manufacturer
authorisation evidence.

## 2. Mandatory Category Attributes

### CAT-004-C3 Required Attributes
Listings shall declare: net quantity, ingredient list in descending order by weight,
allergen declaration, nutrition information per 100 grams or 100 millilitres, storage
instructions, and the shelf-life or best-before basis.

### CAT-004-C4 Allergen Declaration
The allergen declaration is mandatory and shall name every regulated allergen present,
including allergens present through cross-contamination where the manufacturer declares
a precautionary statement. Omitting a present allergen is a Tier 1 safety defect and
results in immediate suppression.

### CAT-004-C5 Ingredient Image Requirement
A legible image of the physical ingredient and nutrition panel is a mandatory secondary
image under IMG-004-C3. Listings without it are suppressed.

## 3. Shelf Life

### CAT-004-C6 Minimum Remaining Shelf Life
Items shall be dispatched with at least 60 percent of total shelf life remaining, or 90
days remaining, whichever is shorter. Dispatching below this threshold is a fulfilment
defect.

### CAT-004-C7 Date Marking
The listing shall state whether the date mark is a "use by" or "best before" date.
Items past a "use by" date may not be listed or dispatched under any circumstance.

## 4. Claims

### CAT-004-C8 Prohibited Health Claims
Claims that a food or supplement treats, prevents, cures, or diagnoses a disease are
prohibited without exception. This includes implied claims through imagery, product
naming, or customer testimonial excerpts in the description.

### CAT-004-C9 Permitted Nutrition Claims
Nutrition claims such as "high in fibre" or "source of protein" are permitted only where
the product meets the quantitative condition defined in the storefront jurisdiction's
nutrition claim register, and the listing states the qualifying value.

### CAT-004-C10 Supplement Dosage Disclosure
Supplements shall state the serving size, servings per container, and the quantity of
each active ingredient per serving. Proprietary blends shall disclose total blend weight
and the ingredients in descending order.
