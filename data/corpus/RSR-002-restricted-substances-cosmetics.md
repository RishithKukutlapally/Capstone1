# Restricted and Prohibited Substances in Cosmetics

## 1. Prohibited Substances

### RSR-002-C1 Absolute Prohibitions
The following are prohibited in cosmetics at any concentration: mercury and its
compounds, hydroquinone in skin-lightening products, corticosteroids, tretinoin,
chloroform, and lead acetate. Detection is a Tier 1 defect with immediate suppression and
mandatory incident reporting under SAF-001-C10.

### RSR-002-C2 Skin-Lightening Products
Skin-lightening products are a high-risk sub-category. Every listing requires a test
report under RSR-001-C8 covering mercury, hydroquinone, and corticosteroids before
approval is granted.

## 2. Concentration-Limited Substances

### RSR-002-C3 Preservatives
Methylisothiazolinone is limited to 0.0015 percent in rinse-off products and is
prohibited in leave-on products. Formaldehyde-releasing preservatives are limited to
0.2 percent expressed as free formaldehyde, and a product exceeding 0.05 percent shall
carry the statement "contains formaldehyde".

### RSR-002-C4 Hydrogen Peroxide
Hydrogen peroxide, present or released, is limited to 0.1 percent in oral products,
4 percent in hair products, and 2 percent in nail hardeners. Tooth whitening products
between 0.1 and 6 percent may be supplied only to dental practitioners.

### RSR-002-C5 Alpha Hydroxy Acids
Free acid concentration is limited to 10 percent for leave-on products with a pH not
below 3.5. Products above this limit are professional-use only and may not be listed for
consumer sale.

### RSR-002-C6 Salicylic Acid
Salicylic acid is limited to 3 percent in rinse-off hair products and 2 percent in other
products, and is prohibited in products for children under 3 years except in shampoos.

## 3. Allergens and Labelling

### RSR-002-C7 Fragrance Allergen Declaration
The 26 designated fragrance allergens shall be named individually in the ingredient list
where present above 0.001 percent in leave-on products or 0.01 percent in rinse-off
products.

### RSR-002-C8 Hair Dye Warnings
Oxidative hair dyes shall carry the allergy alert statement, the instruction not to use
on under-16s, the instruction not to use if the user has previously experienced a
reaction to a hair colourant, and the 48-hour allergy alert test instruction. All four
statements are mandatory in the listing under CAT-005-C6.

### RSR-002-C9 Nanomaterials
Ingredients present in nanoform shall be identified in the ingredient list with the
suffix "(nano)".

### RSR-002-C10 Animal Testing
Products tested on animals for cosmetic purposes after the storefront jurisdiction's
cut-off date may not be listed, and any cruelty-free claim requires a named certification
scheme under CLS-002-C9.
