# Flammability Requirements for Textiles and Furnishings

## 1. Children's Sleepwear

### SAF-005-C1 Scope of Sleepwear Rules
These clauses apply to nightdresses, dressing gowns, pyjamas, and bathrobes intended for
children from 3 months to 13 years, and to adult nightdresses and dressing gowns.

### SAF-005-C2 Performance or Warning
Children's nightwear shall either meet the low-flammability performance requirement of
the applicable standard, or, where it does not, carry the permanent warning label
"KEEP AWAY FROM FIRE". Pyjamas that fail the performance requirement shall carry the
warning; nightdresses and dressing gowns that fail the requirement may not be supplied
for children.

### SAF-005-C3 Label Permanence and Placement
The flammability warning shall be permanently attached, legible after the number of wash
cycles specified in the care instructions, and located where it is visible at the point
of sale on the packaging or a swing ticket.

### SAF-005-C4 Listing Reproduction
Where a garment carries a flammability warning, the listing shall reproduce the warning
in the first three bullet features under SAF-001-C7 and shall display the warning label
in a secondary image.

## 2. Upholstered Furniture

### SAF-005-C5 Filling and Covering Requirements
Upholstered furniture and loose covers shall meet the cigarette and match ignition
resistance requirements applicable to filling materials and covering fabrics.

### SAF-005-C6 Permanent Label Requirement
Upholstered items shall carry the permanent flammability label and, at point of sale,
the display label stating that the item meets the ignition resistance requirements.

## 3. Other Textiles

### SAF-005-C7 Fancy Dress and Costumes
Children's fancy dress costumes are treated as toys for flammability purposes and shall
meet the toy flammability requirement, with the rate of flame spread declared.

### SAF-005-C8 Candles and Wicks
Candles shall declare burn time, shall carry the fire safety warning to never leave a
burning candle unattended and to keep away from children and pets, and shall confirm the
wick contains no lead core.

### SAF-005-C9 Camping and Outdoor Textiles
Tents and sleeping bags shall declare the flammability standard met and shall carry the
warning against using fuel-burning appliances inside.

### SAF-005-C10 Substantiation
Flammability test reports shall be retained for the product's commercial life plus five
years and produced within 10 business days of request under CLS-002-C8.
