# Toys and Juvenile Products Category Guideline

## 1. Scope and Approval

### CAT-003-C1 Covered Products
This guideline covers toys, games, and juvenile products including strollers, cots,
highchairs, carriers, and child restraint accessories, intended for users under 14
years of age.

### CAT-003-C2 Category Approval Mandatory
Listing in this category requires the children's toys and juvenile products approval
under CLS-006-C4. The application shall include a current toy safety test report from an
accredited laboratory for each distinct product.

## 2. Mandatory Category Attributes

### CAT-003-C3 Required Attributes
Listings shall declare: minimum age in months, maximum age in months where applicable,
whether the item contains small parts, whether it contains magnets, battery type and
count, and the applicable safety standard reference.

### CAT-003-C4 Age Grading Consistency
The declared minimum age shall match the age grading printed on the physical packaging.
A conflict between the listing and the packaging is a Tier 1 defect.

## 3. Safety Labelling

### CAT-003-C5 Small Parts Warning
Any toy intended for children under 36 months that contains or can generate a small part
shall not be listed for that age group. Where the toy is intended for children over 36
months and contains small parts, the listing shall display the choking hazard warning
required by SAF-002-C4 in the first bullet feature and on the primary image's
accompanying label image.

### CAT-003-C6 Magnet Hazard
Toys containing loose or detachable magnets exceeding the magnetic flux threshold in
SAF-002-C6 are prohibited for users under 14 and require the ingestion hazard warning
where permitted at all.

### CAT-003-C7 Button Cell Batteries
Products containing button or coin cells shall comply with the secured-compartment and
warning requirements in SAF-003-C7. This applies to toys, lights, and novelty items
regardless of the primary category.

### CAT-003-C8 Cords and Strangulation
Juvenile products with cords, straps, or loops shall state the maximum free cord length
and shall carry the strangulation warning where the free length exceeds 220 millimetres.

## 4. Marketing Restrictions

### CAT-003-C9 Prohibited Developmental Claims
Claims that a toy improves intelligence, accelerates development, or produces a
measurable cognitive outcome are prohibited absent a substantiating study identified in
the listing.

### CAT-003-C10 Imagery Restrictions
Product images shall not depict a child using the product in a manner contrary to the
instructions or without required supervision.
