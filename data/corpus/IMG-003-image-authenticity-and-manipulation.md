# Image Authenticity, Manipulation and Generative Content Rules

## 1. Permitted Editing

### IMG-003-C1 Acceptable Adjustments
Permitted editing is limited to background removal or replacement with pure white,
cropping, straightening, exposure and white-balance correction, dust and sensor-spot
removal, and resizing. These adjustments shall not change the product's apparent colour,
finish, dimensions, or included contents.

### IMG-003-C2 Colour Fidelity
The edited image shall represent the product's true colour under neutral daylight.
Saturation or hue shifts that make the product appear a different colour than supplied
are a Tier 1 defect because they mislead the buyer.

## 2. Prohibited Manipulation

### IMG-003-C3 Content-Altering Edits
Adding, removing, or resizing product features, components, or included accessories in an
image is prohibited. Compositing a product into an image where it was never photographed
is prohibited unless disclosed as a render under IMG-003-C5.

### IMG-003-C4 Misrepresenting Scale
Images shall not misrepresent the product's size. Where scale could be misjudged, a scale
reference such as a ruler or a common object shall be included as a secondary image with
the dimensions stated.

## 3. Renders and Generative Content

### IMG-003-C5 Photorealistic Render Disclosure
A computer-generated render may serve as the primary image only where it is
photorealistic, dimensionally accurate, and the listing states in an attribute that the
image is a render.

### IMG-003-C6 Generative AI Imagery
Images produced by a generative model may be used only as secondary lifestyle images,
shall be labelled as illustrative in an on-image caption, and shall never be used as the
primary image or as a compliance label image under IMG-002-C5.

### IMG-003-C7 Prohibited Generative Uses
Generative imagery shall not be used to depict the product itself, to depict test
results, to depict certification marks, or to depict people in a way implying endorsement.

## 4. Enforcement

### IMG-003-C8 Detection and Review
Suspected manipulation is reviewed against the seller's supplied unedited source file.
Failure to produce the source file within 10 business days sustains the defect.

### IMG-003-C9 Repeat Manipulation
Two or more confirmed content-altering manipulations within a rolling 90-day window
escalate to a selling-privilege review under CLS-005-C5.

### IMG-003-C10 Buyer Remedy Independence
Enforcement under this document does not affect a buyer's separate remedy for an item
not as described.
