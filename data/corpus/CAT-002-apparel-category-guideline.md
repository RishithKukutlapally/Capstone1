# Apparel, Footwear and Accessories Category Guideline

## 1. Scope

### CAT-002-C1 Covered Products
This guideline covers clothing, footwear, bags, belts, hats, and fashion accessories.
Children's sleepwear carries additional flammability obligations under SAF-005.

## 2. Mandatory Category Attributes

### CAT-002-C2 Required Attributes
Apparel listings shall declare: fabric composition by percentage, care instructions,
size system, size value, colour, and target gender or unisex designation.

### CAT-002-C3 Fibre Composition Accuracy
Fibre composition shall total exactly 100 percent and shall be stated to the nearest
whole percent. Terms such as "cotton blend" without percentages do not satisfy this
clause and are an attribute defect.

### CAT-002-C4 Size Chart Requirement
Every apparel listing shall include a size chart image mapping the declared size system
to body measurements in both centimetres and inches. Listings without a size chart are
ineligible for featured placement.

## 3. Labelling

### CAT-002-C5 Physical Care Label
The supplied garment shall carry a permanent physical label stating fibre composition,
care symbols, and country of origin. The listing shall not contradict the physical
label; where they differ, the listing is the defect.

### CAT-002-C6 Country of Origin Statement
Country of origin shall be declared per CLS-004-C9 and shall match the physical label.

### CAT-002-C7 Material Claims
Claims such as "genuine leather", "organic cotton", "recycled polyester", or "vegan
leather" require substantiation under CLS-002-C8. Organic and recycled claims require a
named certification scheme and certificate number.

## 4. Restricted Materials

### CAT-002-C8 Prohibited Animal Materials
Products containing fur from cat or dog species, or any material from a species listed
under an international trade convention appendix, are prohibited.

### CAT-002-C9 Azo Dyes and Chemical Limits
Textiles shall not release the restricted aromatic amines listed in RSR-003. Compliance
evidence may be requested for any dyed textile.

### CAT-002-C10 Drawstrings in Childrenswear
Children's upper-body garments in sizes up to 140 centimetres shall not have functional
drawstrings in the hood or neck area. This is a Tier 1 safety defect.
