# Listing Defect Taxonomy and Enforcement Ladder

## 1. Defect Tiers

### CLS-005-C1 Tier 1 Defects
A Tier 1 defect is one that misleads the buyer, evades platform controls, or creates a
safety or legal exposure. Tier 1 defects result in immediate listing suppression at
detection, without a correction window. Examples include prohibited safety claims,
off-platform diversion, missing hazard labelling, and identifier reuse.

### CLS-005-C2 Tier 2 Defects
A Tier 2 defect degrades catalog quality without misleading the buyer. Tier 2 defects
carry a 14 calendar day correction window before suppression. Examples include
capitalisation violations, missing brand element, and incomplete bullet lists.

### CLS-005-C3 Tier 3 Defects
A Tier 3 defect is advisory. It generates a dashboard recommendation, does not suppress
the listing, and does not count toward the enforcement ladder. Examples include a title
between 150 and 200 characters and a missing leading attribute label in bullets.

## 2. Enforcement Ladder

### CLS-005-C4 First Occurrence
A first Tier 1 or Tier 2 defect on a listing produces a notification to the seller
identifying the clause breached and the required correction.

### CLS-005-C5 Repeat Occurrence
Three or more Tier 1 defects across a seller's catalog within a rolling 90-day window
escalate to a selling-privilege review. The review considers the seller's overall
defect rate, correction responsiveness, and category risk profile.

### CLS-005-C6 Systemic Breach
A pattern of the same Tier 1 defect across more than 20 listings is treated as a
systemic breach and may result in category-level selling restriction pending a
remediation plan.

## 3. Appeals

### CLS-005-C7 Appeal Right
A seller may appeal any suppression within 30 calendar days. The appeal shall identify
the clause, state the basis of the challenge, and attach any substantiating evidence.

### CLS-005-C8 Appeal Outcome Window
Appeals are decided within 10 business days. A listing under appeal remains suppressed
during the review except where the suppression was a Tier 2 defect corrected before the
appeal was filed.

### CLS-005-C9 Automated Detection Disclosure
Where a defect was detected by an automated classifier, the seller is entitled on
request to know which clause the classifier evaluated. The classifier's internal score
is not disclosed.

### CLS-005-C10 No Advisory Determination
Enforcement decisions record a catalog policy outcome only. They are not a legal
determination of the product's regulatory compliance in any jurisdiction, and sellers
remain responsible for their own regulatory obligations.
