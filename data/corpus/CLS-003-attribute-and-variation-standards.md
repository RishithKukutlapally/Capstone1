# Attribute Completeness and Variation Family Standards

## 1. Attribute Completeness

### CLS-003-C1 Mandatory Attribute Set
Every listing shall populate the category's mandatory attribute set in full. The
mandatory set always includes brand, manufacturer part number, country of origin,
package dimensions, package weight, and the category-specific attributes named in the
relevant category guideline.

### CLS-003-C2 Attribute Completeness Score
Listings receive an attribute completeness score expressed as a percentage of the
recommended attribute set populated. A score below 70 percent makes the listing
ineligible for featured placement and for inclusion in the curated category browse.

### CLS-003-C3 Prohibited Placeholder Values
Placeholder values are prohibited in any attribute field. Values such as "N/A", "NA",
"none", "TBD", "0", "-", "generic", and "unbranded" are rejected where the attribute is
mandatory. Where a product genuinely has no brand, the reserved value
"No Brand Declared" shall be used with a manufacturer name supplied.

### CLS-003-C4 Unit Consistency
Numeric attributes shall be submitted with the unit of measure declared in the category
schema. Mixing imperial and metric units within one variation family is an attribute
defect and blocks variation grouping.

## 2. Variation Families

### CLS-003-C5 Valid Variation Axes
Products may be grouped into a variation family only along an approved axis: size,
colour, capacity, material, flavour, pack quantity, or fit. Grouping distinct products
along an unapproved axis to inherit a parent listing's review history is prohibited.

### CLS-003-C6 Review Manipulation Through Variations
Repurposing an existing variation family to sell a materially different product is a
Tier 1 defect, is treated as review manipulation, and results in removal of the
accumulated review history from the family.

### CLS-003-C7 Parent Listing Rules
The parent record in a variation family is not itself purchasable and shall not carry a
price or inventory quantity. The parent title shall omit the variant attribute that
distinguishes the children.

### CLS-003-C8 Child Listing Distinctness
Each child in a variation family shall differ from every sibling in at least one
approved variation axis value. Duplicate children with identical attribute values are
merged automatically and the surplus records are archived.

## 3. Identifiers

### CLS-003-C9 Product Identifier Requirement
A valid global trade item number is required for every listing except in categories
granted an identifier exemption. Submitted identifiers are validated against the check
digit and against the brand registry.

### CLS-003-C10 Identifier Reuse
Reusing an identifier previously assigned to a different product is prohibited and
results in immediate suppression of the offending listing.
