# Content Moderation and Prohibited Imagery

## 1. Prohibited Imagery

### IMG-005-C1 Categorically Prohibited Content
Images shall not depict: sexual content involving minors, non-consensual sexual content,
graphic violence or injury, animal cruelty, self-harm, terrorist or extremist symbols,
or hate symbols. Detection results in immediate suppression and account escalation.

### IMG-005-C2 Adult Products
Where a product is lawfully sold in an adult category, imagery shall be limited to the
product itself on a plain background, without human models, and the listing shall carry
the adult content flag.

### IMG-005-C3 Weapons Imagery
Images depicting a weapon aimed at a person, or a weapon in a threatening context, are
prohibited even where the underlying product is a permitted item such as a costume prop
or a sporting item.

## 2. People in Imagery

### IMG-005-C4 Consent and Likeness
A recognisable person shall appear only with a documented model release. Using an image
of a public figure to imply endorsement is prohibited.

### IMG-005-C5 Children in Imagery
Children may appear only where the product is intended for children, only in fully
clothed and non-suggestive depictions, and only with documented guardian consent.

### IMG-005-C6 Medical and Body Imagery
Before-and-after imagery is prohibited for cosmetics and supplements because it
constitutes an unsubstantiated efficacy claim under CAT-005-C7 and CAT-004-C8.

## 3. Certification and Trust Marks

### IMG-005-C7 Certification Mark Accuracy
A certification or conformity mark may appear in imagery only where the product actually
holds that certification and the certificate reference is recorded in the compliance
attributes.

### IMG-005-C8 Fabricated Trust Badges
Self-created badges resembling official marks, such as invented "approved" or "certified"
seals, are prohibited and are treated as a misleading claim.

### IMG-005-C9 Award and Rating Claims
Imagery asserting an award, star rating, or ranking shall name the awarding body and the
year, and shall be substantiable under CLS-002-C8.

### IMG-005-C10 Reporting and Appeal
Suppression under this document may be appealed under CLS-005-C7, except for content
prohibited under IMG-005-C1, which is not appealable.
