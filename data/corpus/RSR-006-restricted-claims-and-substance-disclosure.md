# Substance Disclosure Obligations and Restricted Marketing Claims

## 1. Substance of Concern Disclosure

### RSR-006-C1 Candidate List Disclosure Duty
Where an article contains a substance of very high concern above 0.1 percent by weight of
the article, the seller shall disclose the substance name and safe-use instructions to
the buyer, free of charge, and shall record the disclosure in the compliance attributes.

### RSR-006-C2 Article Basis
The 0.1 percent threshold applies to each article in a complex product, not to the
assembled product as a whole. A single component above the threshold triggers the duty
for the whole listing.

### RSR-006-C3 Disclosure Timing
Disclosure shall be available at the point of sale in the listing and shall not be
deferred to post-purchase documentation.

## 2. Restricted Marketing Claims

### RSR-006-C4 Free-From Claims
"Free from" claims are permitted only where the substance is genuinely absent, the
substance is one a buyer could reasonably expect to be present, and the claim does not
imply that products containing the substance are unsafe when they are lawfully
compliant. "Chemical-free" is prohibited in all cases.

### RSR-006-C5 Non-Toxic and Natural Claims
"Non-toxic" is prohibited unless accompanied by the named standard against which
toxicity was assessed. "Natural" and "pure" require a stated definition and a percentage
of natural-origin content.

### RSR-006-C6 Hypoallergenic Claims
"Hypoallergenic" requires evidence of reduced allergen content relative to a comparable
product and shall not be presented as a guarantee that no reaction will occur.

### RSR-006-C7 Environmental Claims
Claims such as "biodegradable", "compostable", "recyclable", and "carbon neutral" require
a named standard, the conditions under which the claim holds, and the scope of the
product to which it applies. Unqualified environmental claims are prohibited under
CLS-002-C9.

## 3. Enforcement and Guidance

### RSR-006-C8 Evidence Standard
Claims under this document are substantiated by test report, certification, or an
accredited assessment. A supplier's marketing material is not substantiation.

### RSR-006-C9 Withdrawal of Claim
Where substantiation is not produced within 10 business days, the seller shall remove the
claim. Removal of the claim resolves the defect; it does not resolve any separate
substance limit breach.

### RSR-006-C10 Policy Position Only
Findings under this document express the marketplace's catalog policy position. They are
not a regulatory determination and do not constitute legal advice on the seller's
obligations in any jurisdiction.
