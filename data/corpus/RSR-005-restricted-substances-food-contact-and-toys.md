# Restricted Substances in Food-Contact Materials and Toys

## 1. Food-Contact Materials

### RSR-005-C1 General Inertness Requirement
Materials intended to come into contact with food shall not transfer constituents to
food in quantities that endanger health, change the composition unacceptably, or
deteriorate the taste or odour.

### RSR-005-C2 Overall Migration Limit
The overall migration limit is 10 milligrams per square decimetre of contact surface, or
60 milligrams per kilogram of food for articles intended for infants and young children.

### RSR-005-C3 Bisphenol A
Bisphenol A is prohibited in infant feeding bottles, sippy cups, and any food-contact
article intended for children under 3 years. In other food-contact plastics the specific
migration limit is 0.05 milligrams per kilogram of food.

### RSR-005-C4 Melamine and Formaldehyde
The specific migration limits are 2.5 milligrams per kilogram for melamine and 15
milligrams per kilogram for formaldehyde. Bamboo-melamine composite tableware is
prohibited because the plant-fibre filler is not an authorised additive.

### RSR-005-C5 Declaration of Compliance
Every food-contact article shall be accompanied by a declaration of compliance and shall
bear the glass-and-fork symbol or the words "for food contact".

## 2. Toys

### RSR-005-C6 Element Migration Limits
Migration from toy materials shall not exceed the limits for the 19 regulated elements.
The limits differ by material class: dry brittle powdery or pliable material, liquid or
sticky material, and scraped-off material. Scraped-off material carries the strictest
limits, for example 2.0 milligrams per kilogram for lead.

### RSR-005-C7 Fragrances in Toys
The 55 prohibited allergenic fragrances shall not be used in toys. A further 11
fragrances shall be labelled where present above 100 milligrams per kilogram.

### RSR-005-C8 Finger Paints and Cosmetic Toys
Finger paints, modelling clay, and cosmetic toy sets are subject to the strictest
element migration class and shall carry the age grade and the instruction not to be
ingested.

### RSR-005-C9 Nitrosamines
Nitrosamines and nitrosatable substances shall not be present above 0.05 and 1
milligrams per kilogram respectively in elastomer toy parts intended for children under
36 months or intended to be placed in the mouth.

### RSR-005-C10 Evidence
Toy chemical test reports shall accompany the category approval application under
CAT-003-C2 and are subject to the production requirement in RSR-001-C9.
