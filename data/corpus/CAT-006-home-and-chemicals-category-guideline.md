# Home, Garden and Household Chemicals Category Guideline

## 1. Scope and Approval

### CAT-006-C1 Covered Products
This guideline covers cleaning products, detergents, adhesives, paints and coatings,
garden chemicals, pest control products, and air fresheners. Chemical and cleaning
products require the category approval under CLS-006-C4.

### CAT-006-C2 Safety Data Sheet Requirement
The approval application shall include a current safety data sheet for each chemical
product, dated within the preceding 24 months.

## 2. Hazard Communication

### CAT-006-C3 Hazard Pictograms
Products classified as hazardous shall display the applicable hazard pictograms, signal
word, hazard statements, and precautionary statements in the listing bullet features and
in a legible label image. Omitting any required hazard element is a Tier 1 safety
labelling defect.

### CAT-006-C4 Signal Word Accuracy
The signal word shall be the one assigned by the classification: "Danger" for the more
severe hazard categories and "Warning" for the less severe. Downgrading the signal word
is a Tier 1 defect.

### CAT-006-C5 Child-Resistant Closures
Products requiring a child-resistant closure under the storefront jurisdiction's rules
shall confirm the closure type in the attributes and shall not be repackaged into
non-compliant containers.

### CAT-006-C6 Tactile Danger Warning
Products requiring a tactile warning for the visually impaired shall confirm its
presence in the attributes.

## 3. Specific Restrictions

### CAT-006-C7 Biocidal Products
Products making a biocidal claim, including disinfectants and sanitisers, require a
product authorisation number stated in the listing. A biocidal claim without an
authorisation number is a Tier 1 defect.

### CAT-006-C8 Pesticides and Garden Chemicals
Plant protection products may be listed only where authorised for amateur use in the
storefront jurisdiction, with the authorisation number stated.

### CAT-006-C9 Aerosol Products
Aerosols shall declare propellant type, shall carry the pressurised container warning,
and shall be flagged as dangerous goods for fulfilment under CLS-004-C8.

### CAT-006-C10 Concentration Limits
Where RSR-001 or RSR-004 sets a maximum concentration for a substance in a consumer
product, exceeding that concentration is prohibited even where the product is otherwise
correctly labelled.
