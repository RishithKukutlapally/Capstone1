# Primary Product Image Standards

## 1. Technical Requirements

### IMG-001-C1 Minimum Resolution
The primary image shall be at least 1000 pixels on its longest side. Images below 1000
pixels do not support the zoom viewer and the listing is ineligible for featured
placement. Images below 500 pixels on the longest side are rejected at submission.

### IMG-001-C2 Accepted Formats and File Size
Accepted formats are JPEG, PNG, and WebP. The maximum file size is 10 megabytes. Animated
formats and progressive interlacing are not accepted for the primary image.

### IMG-001-C3 Aspect Ratio and Framing
The primary image shall be square or between 1:1 and 4:3. The product shall occupy at
least 85 percent of the image frame.

## 2. Background and Composition

### IMG-001-C4 Pure White Background
The primary image shall have a pure white background with RGB values 255, 255, 255.
Off-white, gradient, textured, and lifestyle backgrounds are not permitted for the
primary image. This is a Tier 2 defect.

### IMG-001-C5 Single Product Depiction
The primary image shall depict only the product being sold, in the quantity being sold.
Props, accessories not included in the purchase, and alternative colourways are not
permitted in the primary image.

### IMG-001-C6 Product Must Be Real
The primary image shall be a photograph or a photorealistic render of the actual product
supplied. Line drawings, sketches, placeholder graphics, and stock images of a similar
but different product are prohibited.

## 3. Prohibited Overlays

### IMG-001-C7 No Text or Graphic Overlays
The primary image shall not contain text, logos, watermarks, borders, badges, colour
blocks, price flashes, promotional banners, or seller branding. Text that appears
physically on the product or its packaging is permitted.

### IMG-001-C8 No Placeholder or Coming Soon
Images stating "image coming soon", "no image available", or similar are prohibited and
result in listing suppression.

### IMG-001-C9 Nudity and Offensive Content
Images shall not contain nudity, sexually suggestive posing, graphic violence, or
offensive symbols. This is a Tier 1 defect and results in immediate suppression.

### IMG-001-C10 Image Rights
The seller shall hold the rights to every image used. Using a competitor's or brand
owner's image without authorisation is an intellectual property violation independent of
the catalog defect process.
