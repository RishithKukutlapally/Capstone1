# Secondary Image, Video and Gallery Standards

## 1. Gallery Composition

### IMG-002-C1 Recommended Gallery Size
A listing should provide between four and nine images in total. Listings with fewer than
three images are flagged as incomplete content and are ineligible for featured
placement.

### IMG-002-C2 Permitted Secondary Content
Secondary images may show alternative angles, the product in use, scale references,
packaging, included accessories, dimension diagrams, size charts, and label close-ups.

### IMG-002-C3 Lifestyle Images
Lifestyle images are permitted as secondary images and may include contextual props,
provided a caption or on-image note identifies any prop not included in the purchase.

### IMG-002-C4 Text Overlays Permitted
Informational text overlays are permitted on secondary images. Overlay text shall be
factual, shall not state price or promotional terms, and shall not obscure the product.

## 2. Compliance Imagery

### IMG-002-C5 Mandatory Label Images
Where a document in the SAF or RSR series requires a warning, ingredient panel, energy
label, or hazard pictogram to be shown, that label shall be reproduced as a legible
secondary image. The image shall be sharp enough that the mandatory text is readable at
100 percent zoom.

### IMG-002-C6 Label Image Integrity
Label images shall be unretouched photographs of the actual supplied label. Recreating a
label as a graphic, or editing a label image to alter, add, or remove information, is a
Tier 1 defect.

## 3. Video

### IMG-002-C7 Video Requirements
Product videos shall be between 10 and 180 seconds, shall have a resolution of at least
720 pixels vertical, and shall not contain price references, promotional urgency, or
off-platform contact details.

### IMG-002-C8 Video Claims Parity
Any claim made in a video is subject to the same substantiation requirement as a written
claim under CLS-002-C8, and prohibited claims under CLS-002-C9 are equally prohibited in
video.

### IMG-002-C9 Accessibility
Videos containing spoken narration essential to understanding the product should provide
captions. This is a recommendation and its absence is a Tier 3 advisory only.

### IMG-002-C10 Third-Party Content
User-generated or influencer content used in the gallery requires documented permission
and shall not present a personal testimonial as a substantiated product claim.
