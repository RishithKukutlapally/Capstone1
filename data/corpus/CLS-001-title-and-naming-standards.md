# Product Title and Naming Standards

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### CLS-001-C1 Applicability
This standard governs the construction of the product title field for every listing
published to the marketplace catalog, in every category and every storefront locale.
Where a category guideline imposes a stricter title rule, the category guideline
prevails over this document.

### CLS-001-C2 Definition of Title
The title is the single-line primary product name displayed in search results, on the
product detail page, and in the seller feed export. It is distinct from the short
description and the bullet feature list, which are governed by CLS-002.

## 2. Mandatory Title Composition

### CLS-001-C3 Required Title Elements
A compliant title shall contain, in order: brand, product line or model designation,
the distinguishing product type noun, and the primary variant attribute (such as size,
colour, or capacity). Omitting the brand element is a Tier 2 listing defect where a
brand exists for the product.

### CLS-001-C4 Character Length Limits
Titles shall be between 20 and 200 characters inclusive. Titles under 20 characters are
rejected at submission. Titles between 150 and 200 characters generate a soft warning
in the seller dashboard because they truncate on mobile search surfaces.

### CLS-001-C5 Capitalisation
Title case shall be used: the first letter of each principal word is capitalised.
Full-uppercase titles, except for genuine acronyms and registered trademarks that are
themselves uppercase, are a Tier 2 defect and are auto-corrected by the catalog
normaliser.

## 3. Prohibited Title Content

### CLS-001-C6 Promotional and Marketing Language
Titles shall not contain promotional claims, price references, shipping terms, or
urgency language. Prohibited examples include "free shipping", "best seller",
"sale now", "limited stock", "cheapest", and "100% guaranteed". Detection of
promotional language is a Tier 1 defect and suppresses the listing from search.

### CLS-001-C7 Seller Identity and Contact Details
Titles shall not include the seller's store name, website address, email address,
telephone number, or any off-platform contact instruction. This is a Tier 1 defect and
triggers immediate listing suppression pending seller correction.

### CLS-001-C8 Symbols and Decorative Characters
Titles shall not contain emoji, decorative unicode symbols, repeated punctuation, or
HTML markup. The characters permitted outside alphanumerics are hyphen, comma, period,
forward slash, ampersand, parenthesis, and the degree sign.

### CLS-001-C9 Keyword Stuffing
Repeating a term more than twice, or appending unrelated category keywords to widen
search reach, constitutes keyword stuffing. This is a Tier 1 defect. Repeat offences
within a rolling 90-day window escalate to a selling-privilege review.

## 4. Remediation

### CLS-001-C10 Correction Window
A seller notified of a Tier 2 title defect has 14 calendar days to correct the listing
before it is suppressed. Tier 1 defects are suppressed immediately and reinstated only
after the corrected title passes automated validation.
