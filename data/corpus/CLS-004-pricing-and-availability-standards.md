# Pricing, Availability and Fulfilment Data Standards

## 1. Price Data

### CLS-004-C1 Price Field Accuracy
The listed price shall be the total price payable for the item excluding shipping and
statutory taxes shown separately at checkout. Splitting an item's true price between the
item price and an inflated shipping charge is a Tier 1 defect.

### CLS-004-C2 Reference Price Substantiation
A struck-through reference price may be displayed only where the item was offered at
that price on the marketplace for at least 28 of the preceding 90 days, or where the
price is the manufacturer's published recommended retail price.

### CLS-004-C3 Currency and Locale
Prices shall be submitted in the storefront's settlement currency. Locale conversion is
performed by the platform; sellers shall not submit pre-converted prices.

## 2. Availability

### CLS-004-C4 Inventory Truthfulness
Declared inventory quantity shall reflect stock the seller can dispatch within the
stated handling time. Persistent cancellation due to unavailable stock is measured as
the pre-fulfilment cancellation rate and is capped at 2.5 percent per rolling 30 days.

### CLS-004-C5 Handling Time Declaration
Handling time shall be declared in whole business days and shall not exceed 10 business
days without a category exemption. Listings exceeding the cap without exemption are
suppressed from search.

### CLS-004-C6 Pre-Order and Backorder Listings
A pre-order listing shall declare an expected dispatch date and shall not be published
more than 120 days ahead of that date. The expected dispatch date is a mandatory
attribute for pre-order listings.

## 3. Fulfilment Data

### CLS-004-C7 Package Dimension Accuracy
Package dimensions and weight shall be the dispatch-ready measurements including
protective packaging. A variance beyond 10 percent from the measured value at the
fulfilment centre triggers a dimensional correction and a chargeback of the shipping
differential.

### CLS-004-C8 Hazardous Goods Fulfilment Flag
Any item meeting the definition of dangerous goods in SAF-004 shall carry the hazardous
goods fulfilment flag with the applicable transport classification. Omitting this flag
is a Tier 1 defect regardless of whether the item is otherwise compliant.

### CLS-004-C9 Country of Origin
Country of origin shall be the country of substantial transformation, not the country of
dispatch or the seller's registered country.

### CLS-004-C10 Import Duty Disclosure
Where an item ships across a customs border, the listing shall indicate whether import
duties are prepaid or payable by the buyer on delivery.
