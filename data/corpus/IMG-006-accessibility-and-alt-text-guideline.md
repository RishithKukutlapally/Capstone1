# Accessibility and Alternative Text Guideline

## 1. Alternative Text

### IMG-006-C1 Alt Text Expectation
Every gallery image should carry alternative text describing what the image conveys.
Absence of alt text is a Tier 3 advisory defect and does not suppress the listing.

### IMG-006-C2 Alt Text Content
Alt text shall describe the visual content factually in no more than 125 characters. It
shall not repeat the title verbatim, shall not contain keywords unrelated to the image,
and shall not contain promotional language.

### IMG-006-C3 Compliance Image Alt Text
For a mandatory compliance label image under IMG-002-C5, the alt text shall transcribe
the warning text so it is available to assistive technology. This is a mandatory
requirement, not an advisory one, and its absence is a Tier 2 defect.

## 2. Legibility for Low Vision

### IMG-006-C4 Overlay Text Contrast
Text overlaid on secondary images shall meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1
against its background.

### IMG-006-C5 Minimum Overlay Text Size
Overlay text shall be rendered at no less than 3 percent of the image's longest side so
it remains legible on mobile surfaces.

### IMG-006-C6 Colour as Sole Indicator
Colour shall not be the only means of conveying information in a diagram; a label, a
pattern, or a shape shall accompany it.

## 3. Structured Content

### IMG-006-C7 Machine-Readable Attributes Preferred
Information required for a purchase decision — dimensions, materials, compatibility —
shall be provided in structured attribute fields and not exclusively inside an image,
because image-only information is inaccessible to assistive technology and to search.

### IMG-006-C8 Size Chart Duplication
The size chart required by CAT-002-C4 shall additionally be provided as structured
attribute data where the category schema supports it.

### IMG-006-C9 Video Captions
Captions on product video are recommended under IMG-002-C9. Where the video is the sole
source of an assembly or safety instruction, captions are mandatory.

### IMG-006-C10 Review Cadence
This guideline is reviewed annually and requirements may move from advisory to mandatory
with 90 days notice to sellers.
