# Label Format, Legibility and Language Requirements

## 1. Legibility

### SAF-006-C1 Minimum Type Size
Mandatory label information shall be printed at a minimum x-height of 1.2 millimetres.
Where the largest package surface is less than 80 square centimetres, the minimum
x-height is reduced to 0.9 millimetres.

### SAF-006-C2 Contrast and Durability
Label text shall contrast clearly with its background and shall be indelible under
normal conditions of use, including expected exposure to moisture and abrasion.

### SAF-006-C3 No Obscuring
Mandatory information shall not be obscured by promotional stickers, price labels,
shrink wrap seams, or the packaging fold.

## 2. Warning Format

### SAF-006-C4 Signal Word Hierarchy
Warnings shall use the signal word appropriate to the severity: "DANGER" for a hazard
that will result in death or serious injury, "WARNING" for a hazard that could result in
death or serious injury, and "CAUTION" for a hazard that could result in minor injury.

### SAF-006-C5 Warning Structure
A compliant warning states the signal word, the nature of the hazard, the consequence of
exposure, and the action the user must take to avoid it, in that order.

### SAF-006-C6 Pictogram Use
Where a pictogram is prescribed it shall be reproduced at no less than 10 millimetres per
side and shall not be redrawn, recoloured, or stylised.

## 3. Language

### SAF-006-C7 Official Language Requirement
All mandatory safety information shall appear in the official language of the storefront
jurisdiction. Multilingual labels are permitted provided each language carries the
complete mandatory information.

### SAF-006-C8 Translation Accuracy
Translations shall preserve the meaning and severity of the original. A translation that
downgrades a signal word or omits a consequence statement is a Tier 1 defect.

### SAF-006-C9 Over-Labelling
An over-label applied to correct or translate original packaging shall be permanently
affixed, shall not obscure other mandatory information, and shall meet the same
legibility requirements as printed text.

## 4. Digital Labels

### SAF-006-C10 Electronic Label Limits
A digital or QR-linked label may supplement but shall never replace mandatory physical
safety information, warnings, or the economic operator identification.
