# Cosmetics and Personal Care Category Guideline

## 1. Scope and Approval

### CAT-005-C1 Covered Products
This guideline covers skincare, colour cosmetics, haircare, oral care, fragrance,
deodorants, and personal hygiene products. Listing requires the cosmetics and personal
care category approval under CLS-006-C4.

### CAT-005-C2 Responsible Person Evidence
The approval application shall identify the responsible person or importer of record for
the storefront jurisdiction and shall provide the product information file reference.

## 2. Mandatory Labelling

### CAT-005-C3 Full Ingredient Listing
Every cosmetic listing shall publish the complete ingredient list using the
international nomenclature, in descending order of weight, with ingredients below one
percent permitted in any order after the one percent threshold. An incomplete or
paraphrased ingredient list is a Tier 1 labelling defect.

### CAT-005-C4 Period After Opening
Products with a durability greater than 30 months shall declare the period-after-opening
value in months. Products with a durability of 30 months or less shall declare a minimum
durability date. One of the two is mandatory; declaring neither is a Tier 1 defect.

### CAT-005-C5 Net Content and Batch
The listing shall declare net content by weight or volume and shall confirm that the
supplied unit carries a batch or lot code enabling traceability.

### CAT-005-C6 Warning Statements
Where the product requires a statutory warning — for example hair dyes requiring the
allergy alert and patch test statement, or aerosol products requiring flammability
warnings — the warning shall be reproduced verbatim in the listing bullet features and
shall be legible in the label image.

## 3. Claims

### CAT-005-C7 Cosmetic Versus Medicinal Boundary
A cosmetic may claim to cleanse, perfume, change appearance, protect, or keep in good
condition. Claims to treat, heal, or prevent a medical condition reclassify the product
as a medicine and are prohibited in this category.

### CAT-005-C8 Prohibited Claim Examples
The following are prohibited: "cures acne", "removes wrinkles permanently",
"anti-bacterial" without a biocidal authorisation, "SPF 100+", "reverses hair loss", and
"dermatologist prescribed".

### CAT-005-C9 Sun Protection Claims
Sun protection factor may be stated only in the permitted category bands, with the
highest permitted expression being "50+". A broad-spectrum claim requires a stated
ultraviolet-A protection ratio of at least one third of the labelled factor.

### CAT-005-C10 Restricted Cosmetic Ingredients
Ingredients restricted under RSR-002, including hydroquinone, mercury compounds, and
specified corticosteroids, are prohibited in cosmetics regardless of concentration
unless an explicit exemption is recorded in RSR-002.
