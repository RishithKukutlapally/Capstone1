# Restricted Substances in Textiles, Leather and Footwear

## 1. Dyes and Colourants

### RSR-003-C1 Azo Dyes
Azo dyes that may release any of the 22 restricted aromatic amines shall not be used.
The limit is 30 milligrams per kilogram for each amine in the finished article.

### RSR-003-C2 Prohibited Individual Dyes
Disperse Blue 1, Disperse Orange 11, Disperse Yellow 3, and Navy Blue colourant as
specified in the restriction register are prohibited in textiles in skin contact.

### RSR-003-C3 Carcinogenic, Mutagenic and Reprotoxic Substances
Substances classified as carcinogenic, mutagenic, or toxic for reproduction category 1A
or 1B are restricted in textile articles in skin contact at the individual limits set in
the restriction register.

## 2. Finishing Chemicals

### RSR-003-C4 Perfluorinated Compounds
PFOA, PFOS, and their salts and precursors are restricted to 25 parts per billion for
PFOA and 1000 parts per billion for the sum of PFOA-related substances in water-repellent
finishes.

### RSR-003-C5 Chromium VI in Leather
Chromium VI shall not exceed 3 milligrams per kilogram in leather articles in contact
with the skin. This limit applies to footwear linings, straps, and leather garments.

### RSR-003-C6 Dimethylfumarate
Dimethylfumarate, used as an anti-mould agent in sachets, is prohibited above 0.1
milligrams per kilogram in the article or any part of it.

### RSR-003-C7 Nonylphenol Ethoxylates
Nonylphenol and nonylphenol ethoxylates shall not be present at or above 0.01 percent by
weight in textile articles that can be washed in water.

## 3. Metals and Components

### RSR-003-C8 Nickel in Fasteners
Zips, studs, buttons, and buckles in prolonged skin contact are subject to the nickel
release limit in RSR-001-C6. This applies to jewellery-style accessories in the apparel
category.

### RSR-003-C9 Lead and Cadmium in Trims
Lead and cadmium in metal trims, appliques, and jewellery components are subject to the
limits in RSR-001-C4.

### RSR-003-C10 Testing Frequency
Textile test reports shall be renewed on any change of dye house, finishing chemical, or
component supplier, regardless of the 36-month window in RSR-001-C8.
