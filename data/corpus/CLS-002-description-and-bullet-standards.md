# Product Description and Bullet Feature Standards

## 1. Scope

### CLS-002-C1 Fields Governed
This standard governs the long description field, the bullet feature list, and the
"what is in the box" field. Title construction is governed separately by CLS-001.

## 2. Bullet Feature List

### CLS-002-C2 Bullet Count and Length
A listing shall provide between three and seven bullet features. Each bullet shall be
between 30 and 255 characters. Listings with fewer than three bullets are ineligible
for featured placement and are flagged as an incomplete-content defect.

### CLS-002-C3 Bullet Content Rules
Each bullet shall describe a factual, verifiable product attribute or use case. Bullets
shall not restate the title verbatim, shall not contain pricing or promotional terms,
and shall not include comparative claims against a named competitor product.

### CLS-002-C4 Leading Attribute Convention
Each bullet should open with a two to four word capitalised attribute label followed by
a colon, for example "Water Resistance: rated IPX7 for 30 minutes at one metre". This
convention is recommended, not mandatory, and its absence is not a defect.

## 3. Long Description

### CLS-002-C5 Minimum Substantive Content
The long description shall contain at least 150 characters of substantive product
information. Descriptions consisting only of a repeated title, a keyword list, or a
generic brand statement do not satisfy this clause.

### CLS-002-C6 Permitted Markup
Only the following HTML tags are permitted in the long description: paragraph, line
break, unordered list, list item, bold, and italic. Script tags, iframes, embedded
media, forms, and anchor tags of any kind are stripped by the sanitiser and their
presence is a Tier 1 defect.

### CLS-002-C7 Off-Platform Diversion
The description shall not contain any instruction, link, QR code, or encoded reference
directing the buyer to complete a transaction outside the marketplace. This is a Tier 1
defect and a selling-policy violation independent of the catalog defect process.

## 4. Claims Discipline

### CLS-002-C8 Substantiation Requirement
Any performance, health, environmental, or safety claim made in the description shall be
substantiable on request within 10 business days. The marketplace may require test
reports, certification numbers, or a manufacturer declaration as substantiation.

### CLS-002-C9 Prohibited Absolute Claims
Unqualified absolute claims are prohibited in the description. Prohibited forms include
"cures", "prevents disease", "100% safe", "non-toxic" without a standard reference, and
"eco-friendly" without a named certification scheme.

### CLS-002-C10 Comparative Claims
Comparative claims are permitted only against the seller's own prior model, and only
when the comparison basis is stated in the same sentence.
