# Restricted Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment

## 1. Hazardous Substance Limits

### RSR-004-C1 Restricted Substances and Maximum Concentrations
In homogeneous materials of electrical and electronic equipment the following maximum
concentration values by weight apply: lead 0.1 percent, mercury 0.1 percent, cadmium
0.01 percent, hexavalent chromium 0.1 percent, polybrominated biphenyls 0.1 percent,
polybrominated diphenyl ethers 0.1 percent, and each of DEHP, BBP, DBP, and DIBP 0.1
percent.

### RSR-004-C2 Homogeneous Material Basis
Compliance is assessed per homogeneous material, meaning a material that cannot be
mechanically disjointed into different materials. Averaging across the whole product is
not permitted.

### RSR-004-C3 Exemptions
Where an exemption applies to a specific application, the exemption reference shall be
recorded in the compliance attributes. An unrecorded exemption is treated as
non-compliance.

## 2. Documentation

### RSR-004-C4 Technical Documentation
The seller shall hold or be able to obtain the technical documentation and the
declaration of conformity referencing the hazardous substance restriction, and shall
produce it within 10 business days under RSR-001-C9.

### RSR-004-C5 Conformity Marking
The product shall bear the applicable conformity marking. Applying a marking without
holding the supporting documentation is a Tier 1 defect and a misleading claim.

## 3. Batteries and Packaging

### RSR-004-C6 Battery Substance Limits
Batteries shall not contain more than 0.0005 percent mercury by weight, and portable
batteries shall not contain more than 0.002 percent cadmium by weight, subject to the
recorded exemptions.

### RSR-004-C7 Battery Marking
Batteries and battery-containing products shall bear the crossed-out wheeled bin symbol
and, where limits are approached, the chemical symbol for the metal concerned.

### RSR-004-C8 Packaging Heavy Metals
The sum of lead, cadmium, mercury, and hexavalent chromium in packaging shall not exceed
100 parts per million by weight.

## 4. Recycling Information

### RSR-004-C9 Waste Electrical Marking
Electrical products shall bear the crossed-out wheeled bin symbol and the listing shall
state that the product must not be disposed of as unsorted household waste.

### RSR-004-C10 Producer Registration
Where the storefront jurisdiction requires producer registration for electrical
equipment or batteries, the registration number shall be recorded in the compliance
attributes.
